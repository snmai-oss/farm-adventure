🌱 How to Play

📖 Read➜🪣 Get water➜💧 Water crops➜👋 Scare pests➜🫧 Word bubbles➜✅ Pick the right word

💻 Computer

WASD / Arrow keys = walk
Click the ground = walk there
E or double-click = interact
Esc = pause

📱 Phone / Tablet

Drag your finger = walk
Tap the ground = walk there
Double-tap an object = interact
Big ✋ button = interact

💧 Water

Walk to the river and interact to fill your can (3 uses). Thirsty crops turn dull, then brown. Water them before they die! (พืชเหี่ยวต้องรดน้ำ)

🫧 Words

Words are hidden inside the crops. Grown crops release word bubbles. Collect the word that completes the sentence. Wrong word = −20 HP.

🐛🐦🐿️ Pests

Worms, crows and squirrels attack your crops. Walk close and interact to scare them away (+5).

💀 Dead crops

A dead crop can be replanted with one water. You lose HP when a crop dies, so keep your farm healthy!
